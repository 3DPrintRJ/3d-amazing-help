Business Settings

Business Settings is divided into three compact areas so occasional tools do not bury day-to-day configuration.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 24, 2026

Business Info

This is the default section. Enter business identity/contact information, regional settings, and sales/invoice defaults.

Location & regional settings

Country, selling currency, time zone, and address fields are grouped together. Country controls address labels and postal/region validation. When Country changes, 3D Amazing may suggest a matching Currency and Time zone, but intentional overrides are allowed.

United States, Canada, and Australia use controlled State/Province/Territory choices. Other supported countries use country-aware labels and postal validation where practical. The interface remains English-only.

Data Tools

Backup, restore/import, export, and spreadsheet templates live here. These tools are intentionally placed before Plan & Support because they protect and move your business data.

Full Business Backup

Downloads safe business settings and current Customer, Catalog, pricing, Quote, Invoice/Receipt, Payment, inventory/fulfillment, and document-email history data as JSON. The backup is generated through the authenticated 3D Amazing server. Practice export/import remains disabled.

Restore Backup / Import from Backup

These actions are visible beside Export Full Backup. Choose the action you want, select a current 3D Amazing JSON backup, and the app checks the file automatically before anything is changed. It then shows the business name and record counts and opens the appropriate Restore or Import options.

Restore All Business Data

Use full Restore for disaster recovery into an empty real business. Type RESTORE to confirm. The restore is transactional/all-or-nothing.

The original business may still exist. 3D Amazing creates new internal record IDs and remaps linked Customers, Catalog items, Invoices, Payments, Quotes, fulfillment, inventory, and other supported relationships to the restored records.

Account and billing state are not restored. Authentication credentials, plan tier, Stripe customer/subscription IDs, billing status, and other account-security fields stay with the current account. A canceled subscription stays canceled.

Import from Backup

Use Import Selected Data when the current business already contains records and you want reusable data from a backup. The initial safe import choices are Customers, Catalog items/Categories/costing/components, and customer-specific pricing.

The import preview shows new versus matching Customers and Catalog items. Matching records are preserved and skipped rather than overwritten. Existing customer-pricing pairs are also skipped.

Historical transactions are not merged into a populated business. Selected-data import does not import Invoices, Receipts, Payments, inventory movements, fulfillment history, or document-email history.

Spreadsheet import

Customer and Catalog imports validate the expected spreadsheet type before inserting records. A Product sheet cannot be imported as Customers and vice versa. Invalid rows are reported instead of being silently turned into generic records.

Catalog imports also create any explicitly supplied Group names in the managed Group list before inserting the items, so imported groups remain controlled after import.

Plan & Support

Shows the current Free Plan or Paid Plan, customer usage/limits, subscription controls when applicable, and the support level included with the plan.

Support: Free uses self-service Help Center and in-app Help. Paid also includes direct support.

Delete Account

Delete Account appears as a compact account action. Clicking it opens the full deletion warning and confirmation instead of permanently occupying the Settings screen.

The modal explains backup/restore, Stripe cancellation, and permanent deletion. Type DELETE to confirm. If Stripe manages an active subscription, 3D Amazing cancels recurring billing before deleting the owned business, Practice, and login.

Back up and verify your data before deleting your account. The deleted account itself cannot be recovered. A valid Full Business Backup is the supported way to restore business records into a new, empty 3D Amazing account later. Account deletion does not automatically create a refund for previously paid subscription time.

If an owned business ever has other members, account deletion is blocked until ownership is transferred or the other members are removed so shared business data cannot be erased accidentally.

Save

Business Info changes are saved through Save Business Settings. Automatic recovery protects in-progress settings changes from stale overwrites.
