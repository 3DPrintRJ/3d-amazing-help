Business Settings

Business Settings is divided into three compact areas so occasional tools do not bury day-to-day configuration.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 23, 2026

Business Info

This is the default section. Enter business identity/contact information, country, selling currency, address, invoice defaults, machine rate, timezone, terms, and notes.

Country and currency

The interface is currently English-only, but business/customer data supports non-U.S. countries. Country affects address/phone guidance, while selling currency controls business-facing money formatting. Subscription billing currency is separate.

Plan & Support

Shows the current Free Plan or Paid Plan, customer usage/limits, subscription controls when applicable, and the support level included with the plan.

Support: Free uses self-service Help Center and in-app Help. Paid also includes direct support.

Delete Account

Plan & Support also contains the permanent account-deletion control. Type DELETE to confirm. If the account has an active Stripe subscription, 3D Amazing cancels it before deleting the account so future recurring charges stop. The owned business workspace, Practice workspace, and login are then permanently removed.

Back up and verify your data before deleting your account. Account deletion permanently erases the login, business workspace, and Practice workspace. The deleted account itself cannot be recovered. A valid Full Workspace Backup is the supported way to restore the business records into a new, empty 3D Amazing account later. Account deletion does not automatically create a refund for previously paid subscription time.

If an owned business ever has other members, account deletion is blocked until ownership is transferred or the other members are removed so a shared business cannot be erased accidentally.

Data Tools

Backup, import, export, and spreadsheet templates live here. These tools are intentionally separated from normal Business Info.

Full Workspace Backup

Downloads safe business settings and current Customer, Catalog, pricing, Quote, Invoice/Receipt, Payment, inventory/fulfillment, and document-email history data as JSON. The backup is generated through the authenticated 3D Amazing server rather than a series of browser table reads, so internal table permissions do not determine whether a supported backup can complete. Practice export/import remains disabled.

Verify / Restore Backup

Select a current 3D Amazing JSON backup to validate it before restoring. The app shows the backup workspace name and counts for Customers, Catalog items, Invoices/Receipts, Payments, Quotes, and inventory records before anything is changed.

Automatic restore is intentionally limited to an empty real business workspace. Type RESTORE to confirm. This is the supported way to restore business records after creating a new account following account deletion.

All-or-nothing restore: the restore runs in one database transaction. If any required record fails, none of the backup is committed.

Authentication credentials, plan tier, Stripe customer/subscription IDs, billing status, and other account-security fields are never restored from the backup. A canceled subscription stays canceled.

Automatic restore supports the current Full Workspace Backup format. For an older unsupported backup, export a fresh backup from the original account if possible or contact support.

Spreadsheet import

Customer and Catalog imports validate the expected spreadsheet type before inserting records. A Product sheet cannot be imported as Customers and vice versa. Invalid rows are reported instead of being silently turned into generic records.

Catalog imports also create any explicitly supplied Group names in the managed Group list before inserting the items, so imported groups remain controlled after import.

Save

Business Info changes are saved through Save Business Settings. Automatic recovery protects in-progress settings changes from stale overwrites.
