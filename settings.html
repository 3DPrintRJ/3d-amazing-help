Business Settings

Manage your business profile, preferences, payments and data.

Business Profile

Open Business Settings → Business Profile & Practice to edit business details or switch between real Business and Practice.

Sales and taxes

Set invoice details, sales tax rates, currency and other applicable selling preferences. Check settings before recording real sales.

Appearance

Accounts with Appearance access can choose among eight themes. Your choice follows you between Business and Practice. Appearance guide.

Payment Processing

Connect and review supported card processors in Payment Processing. Test payments should use the provider's sandbox where available.

Data Tools

Use Full Business Backup for complete workspace backups. Basic Customer/Product CSV exports do not include every relationship or costing field. Review the target carefully before restoring a backup.

Plan and account

View your current plan and account options. Changes to access or billing take effect according to the displayed plan terms.

Save changes

Select the primary Save action, then check the confirmation. Leave unsaved forms with Save & Leave, Discard & Leave or Keep Editing when prompted.
