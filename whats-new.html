What’s New

Recent changes to 3D Amazing, newest first.

Review draft — November 28, 2026 (pending signup release)

November 28 — Upcoming signup improvements

In review, not yet released: required account-owner first and last names, Starter-first plan selection, a shorter plan label, and a signup button that is hidden while registration is closed. Legal agreement acceptance and email verification are part of the planned signup flow. See Getting Started. Public signup remains closed pending release acceptance.

November 26 — Beta testing tasks and in-app reports

Accepted testers now have an eight-task checklist, saved progress, a four-question final evaluation, and an in-app support report form with screenshots or short video attachments. A one-time welcome message explains how to begin. On iPhone, Beta Tasks is in the dark header beside Help, while the bottom menu remains five buttons. Beta Testing guide.

November 21 — Three workspaces and clearer setup

Accounts are prepared with real Business, editable Practice — Sample Data and Practice — Blank areas. First-login setup and the getting-started tour were clarified. See Practice and Getting Started.

November 20 — Reliable Invoice PDF

Print / Save PDF now opens a PDF or offers Open/Download links if Safari blocks the preview. Practice PDFs include sample markings. Details.

November 19 — Clearer Invoice payment terms

Invoice payment instructions now accept spaces, display next to the date, and appear properly in emailed PDFs. Notes sit above the thank-you footer. Preset due dates remain consistent. Details.

November 19 — Less clutter on voided documents

Voided sales with no Event hide Event Assignment. Assigned Events remain visible as read-only history; active sales stay editable. Details.

November 19 — Readable mobile Invoice totals and payments

Invoice item headings and totals now fit phone screens; Payment History uses labeled rows. Prices and payment amounts are unchanged. Details.

November 19 — Voided invoice balances

Voided invoices keep their historical total but show $0 due; Event editing is separate. Details.

November 19 — Clear Invoice and Receipt statuses

The unnecessary Skip to actions shortcut was removed. Voided documents now show a plain VOIDED — canceled status instead of a button-like banner. Customer-facing unit prices no longer show internal pricing-source labels. Invoice totals, payment actions and records are unchanged. See Invoices & Receipts.

November 19 — Document-first Invoice and Receipt layout

Invoice and Receipt details now show the document before their action buttons, with action buttons immediately below. Browser printing uses a paper-specific layout that removes app navigation and mobile scrolling containers. Check the Safari preview before printing or saving. See Invoices & Receipts.

November 18 — Undo an unused Quote conversion

A Draft Invoice created from a Quote now offers Undo Conversion & Delete Draft. The original Quote returns to Open, the Invoice number stays reserved, and the Customer remains saved. The action is blocked if there is protected payment or fulfillment history. Read the Invoice guide.

November 18 — Convert Quote recipients without retyping

One-time Quote conversion now uses a compact dialog: the saved Customer dropdown is followed immediately by Create Customer from Quote & Convert, and Convert with Selected Customer remains available. The recipient name/email are reused, possible duplicates are flagged, and retry preserves a previously created Customer. See Quotes guide.

November 18 — Browse Catalog components without typing

When adding Assembly components or print materials, tap the search field to browse available eligible Catalog items immediately. Type to narrow the list, choose Show 12 more items for more choices, or Hide list to close it. See Catalog & Pricing.

November 17 — Shorter, clearer Help

Help articles now share a consistent white layout and plain links. The Catalog walkthrough uses readable close-ups of its existing labeled illustrations instead of tiny full-panel diagrams. Sign In & Password Help has genuine public-screen screenshots; signed-in guides now include verified Practice screenshots and narrated workflow videos.

November 16 — Search and appearance

Global Search can find Settings & features as well as records. Accounts with access can choose among eight appearance themes, including options with stronger contrast. Search guide · Themes guide.

November 6 — Catalog and Practice

Catalog item entry became more focused. Practice includes expanded sample items. Catalog guide · Practice guide.

October 31 — Sales workflows

New Sale, payment drafts and mobile navigation were simplified. Make a sale.

Automated checks do not replace iPhone Safari testing. The latest Help release is separate from app feature deployment.
