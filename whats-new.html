What’s New

Major capabilities currently present in the web beta.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 24, 2026

Current beta

Navigation & workflow

  • Unified workflow: Customers → Catalog → Create Sale → Invoices → Payments → Reports
  • Matching terminology/icons across Dashboard, desktop navigation, and mobile Menu
  • Guided tour with visual target highlighting and Exit Tour

Customers & international data

  • Country-aware region/postal/phone guidance
  • International addresses and business selling currencies
  • Optional fields remain truly optional
  • Customer-specific pricing and transaction history

Catalog & inventory

  • Type-first Catalog entry with focused tabs instead of one giant form
  • Managed Catalog Groups instead of free-form Group entry
  • Printed-product costing for filament/material, print time, electricity, hardware, packaging, labor, and markup
  • Inventory movements/lots where applicable
  • Assemblies built from reusable Catalog components
  • Permanent links to bulk import/export in Business Settings → Data Tools

Sales & Quotes

  • Draft/final Invoice workflow
  • Quick Sale Receipts, including offline craft-show queueing
  • Live Quote workflow with edit, print, email, and Convert to Invoice
  • Automatic working-sale recovery and stale-copy protection

Customer document email

  • Email Invoice
  • Email Receipt
  • Email Quote
  • Email Payment Receipt
  • One-time recipient address without changing the Customer
  • Offline Quick Sale receipt-email request sent after successful sync

Payments & Reports

  • Full/partial payments
  • Payment Receipt email
  • Customer, Sales Summary, Invoice, Receipt, Payment, Outstanding Balance, Quote, and Catalog/Inventory reports

Business Settings

  • Business Info → Data Tools → Plan & Support tab order
  • Full Workspace Backup with secured server-side export and verification
  • Restore Entire Workspace now remaps internal IDs so the source workspace may still exist
  • Import Selected Data for safe Customers, Catalog/Categories/costing, and customer-specific-pricing merges
  • Compact Delete Account action with full warning/confirmation shown only in the destructive modal
  • Validated Customer/Catalog spreadsheet imports
  • Free Plan / Paid Plan labeling and support distinction

Usability & accessibility

  • Actionable errors/warnings remain visible until dismissed
  • Specific restore/import/database errors are surfaced instead of generic failure text
  • Show/Hide controls on signup, sign-in, and password setup/reset fields
  • Stronger text size and contrast in informational/help panels across the app

Still planned

Planned Separate Orders/Production and other later-stage business workflows.
