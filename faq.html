Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to common questions.

Review draft — November 28, 2026 (pending signup release)

What is the Starter trial?

New public registration is not open yet. When it opens, new verified accounts will start a 21-day Starter trial without a credit card. Starter costs $2.95/month only after you explicitly subscribe; there is no annual Starter plan or automatic trial charge. When the trial ends, view and export your records; subscribe to resume editing. Existing Free accounts retain their existing access: 10 catalog items, 5 saved customers, 10 completed sales per calendar month and 1 craft-show event per calendar month. Accepted beta testers are exempt from the normal plan limits while their approved beta access is active.

What plans are available?

Starter is monthly only. Maker is $9.95/month or $79.60/year; Pro is $14.95/month or $119.60/year. See pricing for the offer details when registration opens. The new release provides Starter with 10 catalog items, 5 saved customers, 20 completed sales per calendar month and 1 event per month; Maker with 25 catalog items, 20 saved customers, 35 completed sales per calendar month and 3 events per month; and Pro with unlimited capacity. Use Business Settings to check your actual usage allowance. These revised Starter and Maker limits are pending the application release. Quotes and unfinished drafts do not count as completed sales. Upgrades are available anytime.

Why is Submit Registration disabled?

Registration is temporarily unavailable. The red notice explains that new registrations are closed; Submit Registration cannot be used until registration opens. Existing users can sign in, and invited beta testers should use their invitation email.

Where is the Beta Testing checklist?

Accepted beta testers can choose Beta Testing in the desktop top bar or Beta Tasks in the dark mobile app header, beside Help. New testers may see a one-time welcome popup. See the testing guide.

Can I report a beta problem without opening email?

Yes. The in-app Send Report to Support form accepts pasted screenshots or selected files (up to three files, 3 MB combined) and links to longer recordings. A 3DA-BETA reference is not a Zoho ticket number. Reporting instructions.

Does completing eight tasks automatically give me six free months?

No. The checklist and final evaluation request a manual review against your accepted tester agreement before the six-month subscription is awarded.

Does Practice affect my business?

No. Sample and Blank Practice are separate from real Business data. Sample examples can be edited or reset.

What's the difference between Type and Category?

Type describes the item; Category is optional grouping. Assembly is a Type.

Invoice, Quick Sale or Quote?

Invoice bills a Customer; Quick Sale records a paid sale; Quote is an estimate. Choose a sale type.

How do I find a feature or theme?

Use Global Search. Appearance is available only when your account has access.

Can I email receipts or invoices?

Yes, for eligible completed documents. You can use a one-time recipient address without changing a Customer.

Can I accept partial payments?

Yes. Use Payments to record each payment and track the balance.

How do I back up my data?

Use Full Business Backup in Data Tools. Full Restore replaces that Business with backup contents; review before confirming. CSV exports are not full backups.

How do I reset my password?

Use Sign In & Password Help.
