Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the current 3D Amazing Sales Manager beta.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 23, 2026

Where is business data stored?

Supabase is the app backend/database. The public website and Help Center do not store the live business workspace.

Does Practice affect real data?

No. Practice is an isolated learning/test workspace.

Invoice vs. Quick Sale vs. Quote?

Invoice starts as a Draft for a Customer and can carry an unpaid balance. Quick Sale records payment immediately and creates a Receipt; Customer is optional. Quote is an estimate that can be edited, printed, emailed, and converted to an Invoice.

Can customer documents be emailed?

Yes. Invoices, Receipts, Quotes, and Payment Receipts have in-app email actions. A saved Customer email can be changed for one message without changing the Customer record.

Can I email a walk-in Quick Sale receipt?

Yes. Enter the buyer's email in Quick Sale without creating a Customer. If you are offline, the address stays with the queued sale and the email is attempted after the sale synchronizes.

How is price chosen?

Customer-specific price, then Customer default discount, then standard Catalog price. A manual transaction price takes priority.

Can optional fields stay blank?

Yes. Optional fields do not require a value merely because you clicked into them. Validation applies only when a value is entered.

International customers?

Yes. The interface is English-only for now, but non-U.S. addresses, country-aware postal/region guidance, international phone numbers, and business selling currencies are supported.

Can I delete Customers/Catalog items?

Archive/restore is preferred. Permanent deletion is limited when history/dependencies exist.

Partial payments?

Yes. Payments can be less than the full Invoice balance.

Save PDFs?

Yes. Customer documents and reports use the browser Print / Save PDF workflow where supported.

Phone support?

Yes. Quick Sale, lookups, and payments are phone-friendly; setup and data-heavy administration are generally easier on a tablet/computer.

Orders/Production?

A separate Orders/Production workflow is not yet a live primary module.
