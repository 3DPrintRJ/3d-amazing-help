Practice Workspace

Learn without changing your real Business records.

See the Business Settings workspace video and screenshots to view the Sample and Blank Practice choices.

Open Practice

  1. Open Business Settings → Business Profile & Practice.
  2. Choose Practice — Sample Data or Practice — Blank.
  3. Use the Practice banner to identify your workspace.

Sample vs. Blank

Sample Practice includes editable example Customers and Catalog items. Blank Practice starts without examples. Both are separate from real Business.

Reset Sample Practice

Return to Business Settings → Business Profile & Practice to reset the sample records. Read the confirmation: resetting replaces changes in Sample Practice, not real Business or Blank Practice.

Open the Practice settings.

Return to Business

Use your workspace control or Business Profile & Practice. Check the active workspace before recording a real sale.
