Practice Workspace

An isolated workspace for learning and testing without changing business records.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 2026

Switch to Practice

Use the Workspace selector in the desktop sidebar and choose Practice.

Practice warning

The app displays a PRACTICE WORKSPACE banner while active. If you open Help from Practice, the Help Center also shows a persistent Practice Workspace banner and keeps that context visible while you browse Help articles.

Isolation

Customers, products, invoices, receipts, payments, reports, and related records are tied to the active workspace. Practice changes do not affect business data.

Reset Practice

To restore the original sample data, use . This opens the actual reset control in Business Settings → Data Tools; the app still requires confirmation before anything is erased.

Return to business

Switch back to your business name before entering real transactions.
