Practice

Learn and test sales and Catalog workflows without changing records in your real Business.

Current beta documentation · Updated November 16, 2026

Switch workspaces

Use the workspace selector in the app or open Business Settings → Business Profile & Practice. Choose Practice — Sample Data, Practice — Blank, or your real Business. Confirm a switch if prompted. Existing unsaved work must be saved or discarded before switching.

Sample Practice versus Blank Practice

Sample Practice includes realistic example Customers, Catalog items and transactions for guided learning. Built-in Sample Practice Customers and Catalog items can be edited. Reset restores the original examples. Blank Practice is a separate sandbox without sample products or sales, although default Catalog Categories are available.

Both Practice areas can contain up to 250 total Catalog items across their Catalog sections; active and archived items count. That is separate from the Free business plan's capacity limits. Practice is not a replacement for a real business.

Practice warning and isolation

The app displays a clearly marked yellow Practice banner. Help also retains Practice context when opened from Practice. Customers, Products, Invoices, Receipts, Payments, Quotes and inventory history are isolated from your real Business. Full Business Backup, import/restore and live seller card processing are not available as real-business actions in Practice.

Reset Sample Practice

While using Practice — Sample Data, open Business Settings → Business Profile & Practice and select Reset Sample Practice. Read the confirmation: it replaces the sample records and sample tax rates but retains the specified saved workflow choices. It does not reset your real Business or Blank Practice.

Reset is destructive: Save anything you need from your testing before confirming. Do not reset Practice to solve a temporary login or Catalog-loading error; use the app's secure retry guidance instead.

Appearance and returning to Business

For eligible beta/internal accounts, Appearance themes can be changed in Practice and follow the same account back into Business. Switch back to your real Business before entering transactions that should count toward real sales and reports.
