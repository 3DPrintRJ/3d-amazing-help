Forgot your password?

Recover your 3D Amazing Sales Manager sign-in without changing business records or requiring an administrator to view your password.

Updated November 3, 2026

Request a password reset email

  1. Open Forgot Password (or choose Forgot password? from Sign In).
  2. Enter the email address associated with your 3D Amazing account.
  3. Complete the security verification, if shown, and select Email password reset link.
  4. Check your Inbox and Spam folders. Use the newest reset email and choose a new password on the secure page.

For privacy, the app does not tell you whether an email address is registered.

Reset link expired, invalid, or already used

Reset links expire and are one-time-use. Go back to Forgot Password and request a fresh email instead of reusing an older link. Open the link in the browser where you intend to set the password.

Password requirements

New passwords must be at least 12 characters and include a lowercase letter, uppercase letter, number and symbol. The app also checks for known compromised passwords. After successfully saving, sign in with the new password.

Change your password while signed in

A dedicated signed-in account/security control is planned. Until available, use the Forgot Password email-verification flow to set a new password. This changes your personal login, not the business's data.

Still unable to sign in?

Confirm your email address, look in Spam for the latest message, and check the expiration notice. If you still cannot recover access, contact 3D Amazing Support. Never email your password or recovery link to Support.
