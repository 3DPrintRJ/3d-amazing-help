Create an invoice

  1. Choose New Sale → Invoice.
  2. Select a Customer.
  3. Add Catalog items or allowed custom items.
  4. Review quantities, prices, discounts, taxes and terms.
  5. Save the Draft.

Finalize and send

Review the Draft and finalize it when ready. You can then print, save a PDF or email the invoice. Drafts cannot be emailed.

Record payments

Use Payments to apply full or partial payments to the invoice.

Find invoices

Open Sales History to search, reopen or manage saved documents.
