Appearance & Color Themes

3D Amazing can change the colors of the app without changing customer data, prices, invoices, or payment processing.

Current beta documentation · Updated November 16, 2026

How to find Appearance

  1. Open Business Settings in the app.
  2. Choose Appearance from the Settings categories.
  3. Select one of the theme previews. The new colors apply immediately and the choice is saved to your signed-in account.

Another route: In the app's Global Search, type Theme, Appearance, Color, or Palette and choose the Appearance & color themes Settings result.

Availability: Appearance is currently available to eligible approved beta/internal accounts. If that account is eligible, Appearance stays available while using real Business, Sample Practice, or Blank Practice. A separate Practice plan does not grant it to an otherwise ineligible account. The selected colors follow your signed-in account across workspaces and devices.

Eight available themes

  • Current Appearance: the original app colors.
  • Midnight & Gold: navy, gold, and soft blue-gray.
  • Teal & Copper: teal, copper, and cream.
  • Slate & Evergreen: charcoal, evergreen, and pale mint.
  • Indigo & Coral: indigo, coral, and pale lavender.
  • Ocean & Sand: ocean blue, sandstone, and ivory.
  • Plum & Orchid: plum, orchid purple, and lilac.
  • Graphite & Citrus: charcoal, citrus green, and soft gray.

Required, Optional, and errors

Catalog fields labeled Required use an accent derived from the selected theme. Optional fields have neutral borders. Requirements depend on the kind of item and its pricing method. Errors use separate red validation feedback; a color alone never tells you whether a field is required.

Color vision and readability

Ocean & Sand, Midnight & Gold, and Graphite & Citrus may be useful starting points for some types of color-vision deficiency. No theme has been certified as accessible for every form of color blindness. Choose the appearance that makes text, focus outlines, selected controls, and status messages most distinguishable for you.

Troubleshooting

If Appearance is missing, verify that the account is approved for the beta/internal theme trial, then refresh the app. Being in Practice should not hide Appearance for an eligible account. To undo a new color choice, select Current Appearance. On another device, sign into the same account and return to the app to refresh the saved preference.

For help finding other features, see Global Search or Business Settings.
