Appearance & Color Themes

Change the app's colors without changing business records.

Choose a theme

  1. Open Business Settings → Appearance if your account has access.
  2. Choose a theme. The preference is saved to your account.

Eight available themes

Current Appearance
Midnight & Gold
Teal & Copper
Slate & Evergreen
Indigo & Coral
Ocean & Sand
Plum & Orchid
Graphite & Citrus

Exact theme colors from the application configuration; palette samples, not screenshots.

Required, Optional and errors

Field labels use text as well as color. Error states remain distinct in every theme.

Accessibility

Choose the most readable theme for you. No theme has been certified for every form of color-vision deficiency.

Practice

For accounts with Appearance access, the selection follows the account between real Business, Sample Practice, or Blank Practice.
