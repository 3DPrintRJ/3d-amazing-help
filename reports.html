Reports

Review business activity for selected filters/date ranges and print/save supported reports.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 23, 2026

Report types

ReportShows
Sales SummarySummary metrics, sales documents, and payments.
CustomersCustomer contact list, with optional sales data for the date range.
InvoicesInvoices in the selected range.
Quick Sales / ReceiptsReceipt transactions.
PaymentsPayment activity.
Outstanding BalancesNon-void Invoices with money still owed.
QuotesQuote activity/status.
Catalog / InventoryCatalog summary, inventory on hand, low/negative stock, inventory activity, or assembly cost breakdown.

Filters

Available filters change with the report type. Use customer/status/date/sort controls as applicable, then choose Run Report.

Business dates and currency

Report date defaults follow the business timezone, and monetary values use the business selling currency.

Print / Save PDF and CSV

Use browser Print / Save PDF for printable output. CSV export is available where the report supports it; international Customer exports include country information.
