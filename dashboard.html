Dashboard

Quick Access organizes the app by business task without duplicating lists or summary tiles.

Current beta documentation · Updated October 31, 2026

Sales

New Sale opens the chooser for Invoice, Quick Sale, or Quote. Sales History opens existing Invoices and Receipts, including earlier Quick Sales. Quotes opens quote management, and Payments opens balances and payment history. Use New Sale for a new transaction and Sales History to find one already saved.

Other Quick Access groups

Business Management: Customers, Catalog, Events. Reports & Tools: Reports, Search. Administration & Help: Business Settings, Help & Learning.

Navigation on every device

Phones use bottom navigation for Home, Customers, Catalog, New Sale and Menu. Larger screens use a compact horizontal navigation bar rather than a permanent sidebar. Menu lists the same business groups as the Dashboard. The New Sale chooser still offers Quick Sale directly; removing its duplicate Dashboard shortcut does not remove Quick Sale.

Practice and updates

In Practice, the yellow banner identifies the active Practice area and provides a workspace selector. Switching areas prompts you about unsaved work. Use the Menu for Refresh, What's New and account actions.
