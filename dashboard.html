Dashboard

A quick status view of the active business plus shortcuts into the main sales workflow.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 23, 2026

Status cards

CardMeaning
CustomersCustomer records in this business.
CatalogReusable products and other Catalog items.
Open balanceMoney still owed on open invoices.
Draft invoicesInvoices started but not finalized.

Create menu

  • Invoice — starts a customer invoice.
  • Quick Sale — immediate checkout and receipt.
  • Quote — starts a customer Quote.
  • Customer — opens Add Customer.

Your Sales Process

The Dashboard workflow is Customers → Catalog → Create Sale → Payments → Reports. Saved Invoices & Receipts are reviewed from the Create Sale/Sales area rather than a duplicate primary navigation item. Home/Dashboard is an overview, not a workflow step.

Business

All status totals and workflow links operate inside the active business.
