Shortcuts to the tasks you use most.

Desktop and mobile navigation

On a computer, use the top navigation bar for Home, Customers, Catalog, New Sale, and other areas in Menu. On iPhone and other small screens, the bottom bar has five destinations: Home, Customers, Catalog, New Sale, and Menu. The dark header shows the page title and a ? Help button.

Accepted beta testers also see Beta Testing in the desktop top bar or Beta Tasks beside Help in the dark mobile header. See the testing guide.

Find the right shortcut

The videos use Practice — Sample Data. Follow along in Practice to try the examples, or use Business to enter your own records.

Start a sale

Find existing work

Sales History opens Invoices and Receipts. Use Quotes for saved estimates, and Global Search for a specific record.

Other shortcuts

Open Customers, Catalog, Payments, Events or Reports from the navigation or Menu.

Practice

The yellow Practice banner and workspace selector show when you are using sample data; the separate desktop header status label is no longer shown. Use Practice Workspace to explore with separate sample data.
