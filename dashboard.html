Dashboard

A quick status view of the active business plus shortcuts into the main sales workflow.

Current beta documentation · Updated October 27, 2026

Dashboard: your business overview

The Dashboard is no longer a second Create Sale menu. It shows live customer, Catalog, open-invoice and outstanding-balance totals, followed by the five most recent invoices and Quick Sales in the current business. Select a record to open it or use View all for full history. To record a new transaction, use Create Sale in navigation.

Status cards

CardMeaning
CustomersCustomer records in this business.
CatalogReusable products and other Catalog items.
Open InvoicesNumber of open Invoices.
Outstanding BalanceMoney still owed on open invoices.

Recent sales

Up to five recent Invoices and Quick Sale receipts are shown with date, status, amount and document number. The list is read-only until you select an item. Select View all to open the full Invoice/Receipt history.

Where to create a sale

Use the Create Sale navigation item to choose Invoice, Quick Sale or Quote. The Dashboard is for reviewing activity, not transaction creation.

Mobile Menu

On a phone, Menu shows the current Business or Practice area, links to Settings, and a compact Account & app details section with the signed-in email, version, and build details. What\u2019s New, Refresh, and Sign out remain accessible. Business/Practice switching is now under Business Settings → Business Info.

Business

All status totals and workflow links operate inside the active business.

Open invoices shortcut

The Open Invoices dashboard summary opens the Invoices & Receipts list already filtered to unpaid or partially paid invoices, rather than displaying all documents. You can change the filters on that list.

Practice and update banners

When Practice is active, the yellow banner clearly identifies sample data and provides Return to Business. On small screens the banner uses a compact title and action without overlapping text. After an app update, a compact notice offers What’s New and Dismiss.
