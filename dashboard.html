Dashboard

A quick status view of the active business plus shortcuts into the main sales workflow.

Current beta documentation · Updated October 31, 2026

Home overview

Home shows the active workspace's Customers, Catalog Items, Open Invoices and Outstanding Balance totals. Select a summary card to open the related screen.

Quick Access

Below the totals, shortcuts open Sales & Invoices, Create Sale, Quick Sale, Quotes, Events, Payments, Reports, Business Settings, Search and Help & Learning. Use Sales & Invoices to see recent transactions and older history; Home no longer duplicates a Recent Sales list. On phones, More opens the same Menu available in the bottom navigation. On tablet and desktop, the left navigation remains available.

Open invoices

Open Invoices opens the Invoices & Receipts list filtered to outstanding invoices. Change its filters to see other records.

Practice and updates

In either Practice workspace, the yellow banner identifies the active Practice area and provides a workspace dropdown. Choose Business, Practice — Sample Data or Practice — Blank; unsaved work is protected during switching. In Business, use Business Settings → Business Profile & Practice to enter Practice. A separate update notice may display What's New and Dismiss.

Working across devices

The Home shortcuts are available on phones, tablets and desktop browsers. The bottom Menu remains available on phones from every page. The desktop sidebar remains visible at larger sizes.
