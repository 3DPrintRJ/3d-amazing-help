Dashboard

A quick status view of the active workspace plus shortcuts into the main sales workflow.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 23, 2026

Status cards

CardMeaning
CustomersCustomer records in this workspace.
CatalogReusable products and other Catalog items.
Open balanceMoney still owed on open invoices.
Draft invoicesInvoices started but not finalized.

Create menu

  • Invoice — starts a customer invoice.
  • Quick Sale — immediate checkout and receipt.
  • Quote — starts a customer Quote.
  • Customer — opens Add Customer.

Your Sales Process

The Dashboard workflow matches the app terminology and destinations: Customers → Catalog → Create Sale → Invoices → Payments → Reports. Home/Dashboard is an overview, not a workflow step.

Workspace

All status totals and workflow links operate inside the active workspace.
