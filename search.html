Global Search

Search for an existing business record, a Settings page, or an app feature without first finding the right menu.

Current beta documentation · Updated November 16, 2026

Find records

Global Search still searches the currently selected Business or Practice workspace. Matching record groups include:

  • Customers by name, business, email or phone.
  • Catalog items by name, SKU, Type, Category, description and supported material details or price.
  • Catalog Categories by name.
  • Quotes, Invoices and Receipts by document number, status and customer name, where applicable.

Customers, Quotes, Invoices and Receipts open the specific record. Catalog items open their editor. Desktop users can also double-click supported document rows. Record-opening links preserve your search text, so you can return to the results afterward.

Find Settings & features

Matching navigation choices appear in a separate Settings & features section above record results. Select the result to open that Settings area or feature. Examples:

  • Type Theme, Colors, or Appearance to find Appearance & color themes.
  • Type Sales Tax for the tax-rate settings, or Invoice Numbering for Sales & Document Settings.
  • Type Category to find Catalog Categories, including their management screen.
  • Type Backup for Data Tools & Backups.
  • Type Quick Sale, Reports, Inventory, or Events to find an app feature.
  • Type Reset Practice while in Sample Practice to open the confirmed reset controls.
Permissions are respected: Search shows Appearance only for accounts that have access to it, even when using Practice. The Plan & Account result appears only in real Business, and Reset Sample Practice only when Sample Practice is active. Search results are navigation suggestions; they do not bypass login, subscriptions, or workspace security.

Search suggestions

Start with a short phrase such as a customer name, SKU, invoice number, theme, tax, or backup. The results summary distinguishes business-record matches from Settings and features. A search for a feature does not change business data.

To search instructions rather than the live app, use the Help Center search. For themes, read Appearance & Color Themes.
