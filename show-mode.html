Offline / Show Mode

Quick Sale can record sales when a connection is unavailable.

Before the event

Open Quick Sale while online to load current Catalog items and prices.

Make an offline sale

Complete Quick Sale using the available offline payment methods. The sale is queued on this device.

Reconnect and sync

When internet returns, reconnect and confirm each queued sale syncs once. Receipt emails queued with a sale are attempted only after sync.

Limits

Card processors and other online services need a connection. Do not sign out or clear browser data while sales are waiting to sync.
