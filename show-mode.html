Offline / Show Mode

Show Mode is now the offline-capable behavior built into Quick Sale. The old Show Mode route opens Quick Sale rather than maintaining a separate sales workflow.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 23, 2026

3D Amazing records the sale. It does not process credit cards. Continue using your normal payment processor for card transactions and record the method in Quick Sale.

Before the event

Open Quick Sale while connected. The app caches the active Catalog, current prices, active customers, and customer-specific pricing for offline use on that device.

What works offline

You can record a walk-in or cached-customer sale, add cached active Catalog items, change quantities, record the payment method, add notes, and optionally capture a receipt email address.

What stays online-only

Customer creation, Catalog editing, price changes, Business Settings, reports, imports, and other administration remain online-only. Keeping the offline scope small reduces synchronization risk.

Sync queue

Each offline sale receives a unique client ID and is stored in a durable local queue. The sale is synchronized only once. If a receipt email was requested, email delivery is attempted only after the sale has successfully synchronized and a Receipt ID exists.

Email failures

If the sale syncs but the email fails, the sale remains saved. Open the Receipt later and use Email Receipt to retry. A failed send is never displayed as Sent.

Install / home-screen use

3D Amazing can be installed or added to the home screen on supported devices. Open the app online at least once on the device before relying on it at an event.
