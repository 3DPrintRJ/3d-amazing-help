Customers

Customer records hold contact details, pricing rules, and transaction history.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 23, 2026

First customer

If the workspace has no customers yet, the page explains what Customers are for and offers Add First Customer. You can also import customers from Business Settings → Data Tools.

Add or edit a customer

Use Add Customer to create a record. Contact name, business name, email, phone, address, shipping address, shipping phone, resale certificate, tax-exempt status, notes, and default discount are supported.

Optional means optional. Leaving an optional field blank does not require a value. For example, a blank Default Customer Discount means no default discount.

Country-aware fields

Choose the Customer country first. Region/State/Province labels, postal-code guidance, and phone examples follow the selected country. International addresses are supported even though the app interface is currently English-only.

Find and sort customers

Search by name, business, email, or phone. Filter Active, Archived, or All customers and sort the list. A zero-result search offers a clear way to reset filters instead of looking like an empty database.

Customer detail

Customer detail connects Overview, customer-specific pricing, Invoices & Receipts, Payments, and Quotes/history actions while preserving the selected customer when you start a new sale.

Customer pricing order

  1. Customer-specific Catalog price, if one exists.
  2. Customer default discount applied to the standard Catalog price.
  3. Standard Catalog price.

A manual transaction price overrides automatic pricing for that transaction only.

Archive, restore, and permanently delete

Archive hides a customer from normal new transactions while preserving history. Permanently Delete is restricted to unused records so invoices, receipts, Quotes, and payments cannot be orphaned.
