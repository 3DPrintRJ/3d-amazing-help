Add a customer

  1. Open Customers.
  2. Choose Add Customer.
  3. Enter the required details and select Save.

Edit customer details

Open a customer to update contact, mailing, shipping, tax or optional pricing information. Save before leaving.

Find a customer

Use Customer search or Global Search. Open the Customer record to review related invoices, quotes and sales.

Archive or restore

Archive records you no longer use. Permanent deletion is limited when sales history depends on a customer.

Import or export

Customer CSV/XLSX tools are under Business Settings → Data Tools.
