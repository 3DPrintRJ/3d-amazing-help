Contact Support

Use the Help Center and in-app Help first. Direct support availability depends on the plan.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 23, 2026

Free Plan support

Free Plan support is self-service through this Help Center and the in-app Help/Help Tips system.

Paid Plan support

Paid Plan includes direct support in addition to the Help Center and in-app Help.

When to contact support

Use direct support when the documentation does not answer the question, something appears broken, an email/document action fails repeatedly, or you want to suggest an improvement.

Before contacting support

Check the active workspace, refresh the app, confirm the relevant filters/settings, and review the Help article for the page.

Bug report details

  • Page or feature
  • What you were trying to do
  • Expected result
  • Actual result
  • Exact error message, if any
  • Business or Practice workspace
  • Device/browser
  • Screenshot when useful

Feature requests

Describe the problem to solve and the simplest workflow you expect rather than only naming a feature.

Email

support@3damazing.com
