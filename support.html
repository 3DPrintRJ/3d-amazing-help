Contact Support

Start with the Help Center for instructions, or contact us when something is not working.

Updated November 26, 2026

Find an answer

Use Help Center search or the in-app ? Help button to open instructions for your current page. Help tips provide additional descriptions where available.

Report a problem

Tell us the app area, what you were trying to do, what happened, the steps to reproduce it, and your device or browser. A screenshot is useful. Do not include passwords, payment-card numbers, API keys, or private customer information.

For accepted beta testers

Use Beta Testing from the app's top navigation (or Beta Tasks beside Help in the mobile dark header). The built-in report form sends directly to support@3damazing.com; it does not open your mail app. You can paste screenshots, choose up to three files totaling 3 MB, or include a link for a larger video.

A 3DA-BETA reference confirms the report was accepted by the app's email service. It is not a Zoho Desk ticket number; support-desk ticket creation and delivery are separate steps. If the app reports a send failure, contact Support by email instead.

Suggest an improvement

Describe what you want to accomplish and why. You can also review the Roadmap for feature status.

Contact by email

Email support@3damazing.com from your usual email address. If you are following up on an in-app report, include its 3DA-BETA reference. Do not send sensitive credentials or payment details.
