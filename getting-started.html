Getting Started

Set up your business and make your first sale.

Updated November 17, 2026

1. Sign in

Open 3D Amazing, enter your email and password, then select Sign in. Need sign-in or password help?

2. Try Practice (optional)

Open Business Settings → Business Profile & Practice. Practice lets you learn without changing your real Business. Using Practice.

3. Complete Business Settings

Enter your business name, contact information, region, currency and sales tax settings. Business Settings guide.

4. Add customers and items

Add a Customer when needed. Open Catalog → + Add to create an item; start with the required fields. Illustrated Catalog walkthrough.

5. Make a sale

Open New Sale and choose Quick Sale (paid now), Invoice (bill a customer) or Quote (estimate). Existing invoices and receipts are in Sales History.

6. Record payments and review reports

Use Payments for full or partial invoice payments. Use Reports to review business activity.

Need help finding something?

Use Global Search for records, Settings and app features. Use the Help Center search for instructions.
