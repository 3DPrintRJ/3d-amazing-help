Getting Started

3D Amazing is organized around a real business workspace plus an isolated Practice workspace for learning and testing.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 23, 2026

Recommended first-time order: Business Settings → Customers → Catalog → Create Sale → Invoices → Payments → Reports.
Works on phones, best on a larger screen for setup. Phones are well suited to Quick Sales, customer lookups, payments, and craft-show use. Catalog setup, reports, imports, and longer sessions are easier on a tablet or computer.

1. Sign in and choose a workspace

After signing in, the app opens your active workspace. Your real business data stays separate from Practice, which contains sample data for learning and testing.

2. Complete Business Settings

Open Business Settings → Business Info and enter your business identity, country, selling currency, contact information, address, invoice defaults, machine rate, timezone, terms, and notes. Country and currency are used throughout customer addresses, money formatting, and business dates.

3. Add customers

Customers can include contact/business names, email, phone, country-aware mailing and shipping addresses, optional default discount, resale/tax information, and notes. Optional fields may be left blank.

4. Build the Catalog

Add the products, printed parts, materials, hardware, packaging, labor/services, and assemblies you use. Catalog items can carry selling prices, costing information, and inventory settings.

5. Create a sale or Quote

Invoice

Requires a customer and starts as a Draft. Finalize it when ready.

Quick Sale

Customer is optional. It records payment immediately, creates a receipt, and can optionally email that receipt.

Quote

Creates a customer estimate that can be printed, emailed, edited, and converted to an Invoice.

Unfinished Invoice and Quick Sale work is saved automatically so it can be recovered safely. The app protects newer work from being overwritten by a stale tab or device.

6. Craft-show / offline Quick Sale

Open Quick Sale online before the event so active customers, Catalog items, prices, and customer-specific pricing are cached. Offline sales are stored on the device and synchronized when internet returns. If you enter a receipt email while offline, the address stays with the queued sale and the email is attempted only after that sale successfully syncs.

7. Invoices, Payments, and Reports

Use Invoices to manage sales documents, Payments for full or partial payments, and Reports to review Customers, Invoices, Receipts, Payments, balances, Quotes, and Catalog/Inventory activity.

Email customer documents

Invoices, Receipts, Quotes, and Payment Receipts can be emailed from inside 3D Amazing. A saved customer email is prefilled when available, but you may use a one-time address without changing the Customer record.

App navigation

On desktop, the left navigation follows the working modules directly. On phones, the permanent navigation is Home, Customers, Catalog, Create Sale, Menu. The Menu contains Invoices, Payments, Reports, Business Settings, Search, version information, Refresh App, and Sign Out.

Help and your plan

The dark app header contains your Free Plan, Paid Plan, or Practice Sandbox badge and the Help button. Help opens guidance for the page you are using.
