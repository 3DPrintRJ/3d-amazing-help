Getting Started

Set up your business and make your first sale.

Review draft — November 28, 2026 (pending signup release)

Create your account (when registration opens)

New public registration is currently closed. When it opens, go to the Create your account screen. Accepted beta testers should use their invitation instead.

  1. Choose a plan. Starter is listed first and costs $2.95/month if you decide to subscribe; it has no annual option.
  2. Enter your first and last name, Business name and Business country.
  3. Enter and confirm your email and password. Use at least 12 characters, including uppercase and lowercase letters, a number and a symbol.
  4. Read and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, complete security verification, then select Create My Account. The button is not shown while signup is closed.
  5. Open the confirmation email, verify your email address, sign in and complete agreement acceptance. Your 21-day Starter trial begins when your verified Business is created. No card is required and no automatic trial payment is taken.

After the trial ends, your existing records can be viewed and exported; subscribe to resume editing. See FAQ.

1. Sign in

Open 3D Amazing, enter your email and password, then select Sign in. Need sign-in or password help?

2. Find your way around

On a computer, the main navigation is across the top. On a phone, the five main destinations are along the bottom, with additional features in Menu. ? Help opens help for the current page. Accepted beta testers also have Beta Testing in the top menu (or Beta Tasks in the dark mobile header). Beta Testing instructions.

3. Try Practice (optional)

Open Business Settings → Business Profile & Practice. Practice lets you learn without changing your real Business. Using Practice.

4. Complete Business Settings

Enter your business name, contact information, region, currency and sales tax settings. Business Settings guide.

5. Add customers and items

Add a Customer when needed. Open Catalog → + Add to create an item; start with the required fields. Illustrated Catalog walkthrough.

6. Make a sale

Open New Sale and choose Quick Sale (paid now), Invoice (bill a customer) or Quote (estimate). Existing invoices and receipts are in Sales History.

7. Record payments and review reports

Use Payments for full or partial invoice payments. Use Reports to review business activity.

Need help finding something?

Use Global Search for records, Settings and app features. Use the Help Center search for instructions.
