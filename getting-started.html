Getting Started

3D Amazing is organized around your real business plus an isolated Practice area for learning and testing.

Current beta documentation · Updated October 27, 2026

Recommended first-time order: Business Settings → Customers → Catalog → New Sale → Payments → Reports. Saved Invoices & Receipts are opened from Sales History.
Works on phones, best on a larger screen for setup. Phones are well suited to Quick Sales, customer lookups, payments, and craft-show use. Catalog setup, reports, imports, and longer sessions are easier on a tablet or computer.

Finding your parts before building Assemblies

On the Catalog page choose Products for finished items and completed sellable Assemblies, 3D Printed Assembly Parts for printed components not sold separately, or Supplies & Services for materials, purchased hardware, packaging and labor. You can create 3D Printed Assembly Parts before assigning them to any Assembly. Linking a part later will not require recreating the Catalog record. The tabs filter existing records; they do not change stock, price or tax.

Loading errors are not empty data

The Catalog distinguishes a genuinely empty business from failed authenticated requests. An authentication timestamp error may require correcting device date and time or refreshing the session. The secure retry action does not delete Catalog data or reset workspaces. Do not clear browser data if you may have unfinished work.

Mobile Catalog inventory workflow

For inventory tasks on phones, tap the Catalog item and switch to Inventory in its focused editor. Stock updates, assembly building, and history are available there, rather than below the compact Catalog row. Unsaved product edits must be resolved before changing live stock.

Using mobile Catalog lists

On a phone, Products and Supplies & Services are searchable directories rather than stacks of large product cards. Select a row to edit its complete record. Use Filters & Sort for archived products, stock, Type, Category and sort order. The full list can scroll normally; no product is removed from results to reduce screen height.

Expandable explanations without hiding work

Nonessential explanations use short How it works sections that open on demand. Required entry, validation messages, save failures, Practice warnings, inventory problems, payment state and destructive-action warnings remain visible. Optional notes announce when they contain entered information.

Compact mobile navigation

Phones now have a slim branded header with the current page and a small contextual Help button. The five main navigation choices stay accessible at the bottom of the screen, with the secondary sections in Menu. Plan status appears in Business Settings → Plan & Account. Mobile Use Guide and Take Tour Again have moved to Business Settings → Help & Learning. The Dashboard groups shortcuts by task; New Sale lets you choose Invoice, Quick Sale, or Quote.

A simpler navigation strategy

On desktop, the permanent sidebar has been replaced by a horizontal navigation bar. On phones, Search and the main action remain visible when practical; use Filters & sort or Report options to reveal less-frequently changed controls. Large secondary forms open in focused windows, with Apply/Save and Cancel separated. Desktop retains tables and record details. Lists and long forms may still scroll, but users should not have to hunt below unrelated panels for the next action.

Help, browsers and the installed app

3D Amazing works in regular browser tabs and can also be added to an iPhone or Android Home Screen. In a normal browser, selecting Help opens the matching Help Center article in the same tab. The Back to 3D Amazing link returns to the page you came from, even after exploring other Help articles. If a form has unsaved changes, the app may ask you to save or confirm before leaving.

When you use a separately installed Home Screen app, Help may open in Safari or your normal browser. To return, switch back to the 3D Amazing app using its icon or your app switcher. Do not open another browser copy of 3D Amazing merely to exit Help. Browser-specific differences are tested and documented before beta releases.

1. Sign up or sign in

3D Amazing can accept a limited number of public beta signups when the current signup group is open. The signup page shows whether openings remain. Existing invited beta users can continue signing in normally. After email verification and sign-in, the app creates the Free business workspace and keeps it separate from Practice, which contains sample data for learning and testing.

Try Practice without risking business data

Open Business Settings → Business Info and choose Switch to Practice to learn using isolated sample data. Confirm before switching; the app will reload. The Practice banner identifies sample data. Return from the same Business Info section using Return to Business. The redundant Current Area navigation block has been removed. In Practice, a yellow SAMPLE DATA banner stays visible with Return to Business. Switch work areas using Business Settings → Business Info.

2. Complete Business Settings

Open Business Settings → Business Info and enter your business identity, country, selling currency, contact information, address, invoice defaults, machine rate, timezone, terms, and notes. Country and currency are used throughout customer addresses, money formatting, and business dates.

3. Add customers

Customers can include contact/business names, email, phone, country-aware mailing and shipping addresses, optional default discount, resale/tax information, and notes. Optional fields may be left blank.

4. Build the Catalog

Choose an item type first, then complete only the relevant tabs. Printed products have a manufacturing-cost workflow; Assemblies use reusable components; purchased materials and services use their own focused cost/inventory fields. Catalog Groups come from a managed list so names stay consistent.

5. Create a sale or Quote

Invoice

Requires a customer and starts as a Draft. Finalize it when ready.

Quick Sale

Customer is optional. It records payment immediately, creates a receipt, and can optionally email that receipt.

Quote

Creates a customer estimate that can be printed, emailed, edited, and converted to an Invoice.

Unfinished Invoice and Quick Sale work is saved automatically so it can be recovered safely. The app protects newer work from being overwritten by a stale tab or device.

6. Craft-show / offline Quick Sale

Open Quick Sale online before the event so active customers, Catalog items, prices, and customer-specific pricing are cached. Offline sales are stored on the device and synchronized when internet returns. If you enter a receipt email while offline, the address stays with the queued sale and the email is attempted only after that sale successfully syncs.

7. Invoices, Payments, and Reports

From Create Sale, use View Invoices & Receipts to manage saved sales documents. Use Payments for full or partial payments and Reports to review Customers, Invoices, Receipts, Payments, balances, Quotes, and Catalog/Inventory activity.

Email customer documents

Invoices, Receipts, Quotes, and Payment Receipts can be emailed from inside 3D Amazing. A saved customer email is prefilled when available, but you may use a one-time address without changing the Customer record.

App navigation

On desktop, the left navigation keeps the main workflow compact. On smaller desktop windows, scroll within the left sidebar to reach all options; the current Business/Practice indicator and navigation links remain in separate, nonoverlapping rows. Saved Invoices & Receipts are reached from Create Sale → View Invoices & Receipts. On phones, the permanent navigation is Home, Customers, Catalog, Create Sale, Menu. The Menu contains Payments, Reports, Business Settings, Search, version information, Refresh App, and Sign Out.

Required app updates

When a newer build is available, 3D Amazing displays a foreground Refresh App prompt until the update is applied. Refreshing checks for unsaved changes first and may ask you to save or discard your work.
