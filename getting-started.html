Getting Started

The current beta is organized around a business workspace, with an optional isolated Practice workspace for learning and testing.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 2026

Recommended first-time order: Business Settings → Customers → Products → Create Sale.
Works on phones, best on a larger screen. Phones are well suited to quick sales, customer lookups, and payments. For setup, product management, reports, and longer work sessions, we recommend a tablet or computer.

1. Sign in and choose a workspace

After signing in, the app opens your active workspace. The desktop sidebar includes a Workspace selector. Most accounts have a regular business workspace plus a Practice workspace containing isolated sample data.

2. Complete Business Settings

Open Business Settings and enter the business name, owner/contact information, address, invoice prefix, default machine rate, timezone, invoice terms, and default invoice notes. These values are reused throughout the app.

3. Add customers

Customers can include contact and business names, email, phone, mailing and shipping addresses, default discount, resale certificate information, tax-exempt status, and notes. You can add a customer from the Customers page or while creating an invoice or Quick Sale.

4. Add products

Create reusable catalog products with SKU, description, and standard price. The built-in 3D-print costing calculator can estimate material, machine, hardware, packaging, and labor cost and then suggest a retail price.

5. Create a transaction

Create Invoice

Requires a customer and creates a Draft invoice. Finalize it when ready.

Quick Sale

Customer is optional. It records payment immediately and creates a receipt.

Unfinished sales are saved on the server as you work. If you close the browser, switch devices, or open another tab, 3D Amazing can recover the active sale. Only one working sale is active per workspace at a time.

6. Use Show Mode when needed

For craft shows or unreliable internet, open Show Mode while online first and refresh its offline data. Show Mode records simple sales and queues them safely for synchronization; it does not process credit cards.

7. Record payments and run reports

Use Payments for full or partial payments against open invoices. Reports can show sales summaries, invoices, receipts, payments, and outstanding balances for a selected date range.

App navigation

Desktop navigation appears in the left sidebar. On phones, the main navigation is Home, Create Sale, Customers, Products, and Menu. The Menu contains Help, Show Mode, Reports, Business Settings, Search, version information, Refresh App, and Sign Out. Balloon-style field tips are disabled on phones; use the Help button for page guidance.
