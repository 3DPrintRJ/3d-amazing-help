Account Administration

Administrative controls are restricted to authorized accounts.

Users and access

Use authorized account tools to review memberships, plans and account status. Do not share or export another person's private information.

Beta Tester Progress (founder)

Open Admin → Beta Progress in the app, or use Beta Tester Progress while signed in as the founder. This page lists accepted beta businesses and a separately labeled Founder Test account; the founder entry does not count toward reward eligibility.

Checklist shows reviewed tasks out of eight, Problems marked counts task responses indicating a problem, and Final evaluation indicates whether written feedback was submitted. Open Read feedback for the four responses. These values do not guarantee every workflow passed; review the tester's descriptions.

Reports sent from the app lists internal 3DA-BETA references and email-provider acceptance. It does not verify delivery to Zoho Desk. Review incoming support tickets separately before confirming beta completion or granting the six-month reward. See Beta Testing instructions.

Security

Use only authorized testing tools and test accounts. Never publish secrets, passwords or session tokens.

Support

For account-related issues, use Support.
