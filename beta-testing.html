Beta Testing: Your Checklist & Reports

For accepted beta testers. Work through eight short tasks, report anything confusing or broken, and share a final evaluation. Your invitation specifies the deadline, usually about ten days.

Updated November 26, 2026

Find your testing tasks

  1. Sign in to 3D Amazing with your accepted tester account.
  2. On a computer, choose Beta Testing in the top menu. On a phone, choose Beta Tasks in the dark header beside the Help ? button.
  3. First-time testers may see a one-time welcome message explaining the checklist. Choose Open Beta Testing, or open the menu button whenever you need it.

Open Beta Testing in the app. The tasks page is only available to accepted testers and the founder.

Use Practice — Sample Data

Choose Practice — Sample Data using the yellow workspace selector before testing sales, payments, or inventory. This workspace is separate from your real Business. Your beta checklist progress is saved to your accepted Business account even while you are working in Practice.

See Practice Workspace for switching between Sample, Blank and Business. Avoid making test transactions in your real Business.

Complete the checklist

The eight areas are Customers; Products & Inventory; Quotes & Invoices; Quick Sale; Payments; Events; Reports; and a Different Device where available. Open the relevant area from each task and try the instruction.

For each task choose Done, Had a problem, or Not applicable. The selection saves automatically. Choosing Had a problem still counts as reviewing the task; report the issue using the form below. Progress is not the same as approval of the final reward.

Send a bug report or suggestion

  1. On the Beta Testing page, scroll to Report a problem or suggestion.
  2. Choose the area, write a short title and describe what happened. Steps to reproduce are optional but useful.
  3. On a computer, paste a screenshot into the form using Ctrl+V or Command+V. You can also drag files into the form or use Choose Files; on a phone, use Choose Files.
  4. Attach up to 3 files with a combined maximum size of 3 MB. Supported files include PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, HEIC, MP4, MOV and WebM. For a longer video, paste a secure HTTPS sharing link instead.
  5. Select Send Report to Support. The form sends inside 3D Amazing; it does not open an email app.

If the email service accepts your report, the app displays a reference such as 3DA-BETA-…. This is an application report reference, not a Zoho Desk ticket number, and does not alone confirm a support ticket was created. If the app reports an error, do not assume the report was delivered. Contact support@3damazing.com if needed.

Send screenshots that show the issue, but never attach passwords, payment-card data, private customer records, or credentials. See Contact Support for other assistance.

Submit your final evaluation

After marking all eight tasks, answer four short questions: what you liked, what you disliked, what was confusing, and what should be improved. Enter None where appropriate, then choose Submit final evaluation.

Submission requests a manual completion review. The complimentary six-month subscription is awarded only to accepted testers who fulfill the testing agreement; it is not activated merely by checking eight boxes.
