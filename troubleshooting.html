Troubleshooting

First checks for common beta problems.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 2026

Update available

Choose Refresh app in the desktop sidebar/footer or mobile Menu.

Wrong or missing data

  1. Check the active Workspace and make sure you are not in Practice.
  2. Refresh the app.
  3. Check filters such as Active/Archived, date range, type, or status.
  4. Use Search to look for the record.

Cannot sign in

Confirm the correct email/password. Temporary beta passwords may require a password change on first login.

Archived record disappeared

Change the View filter to Archived or All, then restore it if needed.

Cannot permanently delete

Customers with transaction history and products used on sales documents are protected from permanent deletion.

Unexpected price

Pricing order is customer-specific price → customer discount → standard price. A manual transaction override takes priority.
