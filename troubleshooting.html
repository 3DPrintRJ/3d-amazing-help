Troubleshooting

Common fixes for sign-in, saved records, email and syncing.

Can't sign in?

Check your email and password. Use Forgot password? if needed.

Outdated screen or update prompt

Use Refresh App when prompted. Save unfinished changes first.

Record missing?

Check the active Business or Practice workspace and the search/Archived filters. Do not reset your workspace to find missing data.

Save failed?

Check the error message and connection. Confirm the record saved before closing the editor.

Email didn't arrive?

Verify the address and check Spam. Review the send status before retrying.

Offline sale won't sync?

Reconnect, reopen Quick Sale and review queued sales. Avoid signing out or clearing browser data until they sync.

Backup or restore problem?

Check the backup type and target workspace. A Full Business Backup is not the same as Customer/Product CSV.

Beta Testing report will not send?

Confirm you are signed into your accepted beta account, then check that the report has a title and description. You can paste a screenshot or attach up to three files totaling 3 MB. For a larger video, paste a secure HTTPS sharing link instead. A send failure means the report was not confirmed; try again or email support@3damazing.com.

The 3DA-BETA confirmation is a report reference from 3D Amazing, not a Zoho Desk ticket number. See Beta Testing for the complete workflow.

Still stuck?

Contact Support with the screen, error and steps to reproduce it.
