3D Amazing Sales Manager

Product Roadmap

A clear look at what 3D Amazing does today and what we're working toward for makers, crafters, and small product sellers.

Last reviewed: October 14, 2026 · Version 1.1 Beta

During our invitation-only beta, we're prioritizing reliable everyday selling. Planned features are not yet available, and the order may change based on testing and customer feedback. This is a product direction, not a release-date promise.

Available nowPlanned nextFutureDeferred

Working features

Available now

In the beta

Catalog, pricing & inventory

Manage products, prices, costing, assemblies, stock levels, and archived items.

Customers & sales documents

Manage customers and create Quick Sales, receipts, invoices, and quotes with discounts and tax calculations.

Payments & receipts

Record payments and send or print documents. Square and Stripe seller-payment integrations are being tested in the controlled beta; availability depends on connected account and supported setup.

Events & craft-show workflows

Organize sales by event, record event expenses, and use offline Show Mode for supported Quick Sale workflows.

Reports & sales tax

Review sales, payments, customers, events, and sales tax charged by component. Existing reports are not a tax filing service.

Data tools & Practice

Import and export supported Catalog/customer data, back up the business, and use a separate Practice area.

Highest priorities

Planned next

Not released

Etsy shop integration

Connect an Etsy shop, match listings to Catalog products, and import marketplace orders, fees, refunds, and tax information with duplicate protection.

PayPal seller payments

Offer PayPal as an optional payment method alongside a seller's existing card processor, subject to provider requirements and testing.

Better tax exports

Download accountant-friendly sales and tax details, including discounts, payment methods, refunds, and marketplace-collected tax.

Schedule C Tax Prep Packet

Prepare business sales and expense summaries to help sellers organize records for a tax professional. Not tax filing or advice.
