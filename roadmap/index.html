The road so far

Features available now

Core features included in the current 3D Amazing beta. Some payment integrations depend on account setup and testing.

Catalog, pricing & inventory

Manage products, prices, costing, assemblies, stock levels, and archived items.

Customers & sales documents

Manage customers and create Quick Sales, receipts, invoices, and quotes with discounts and tax calculations.

Payments & receipts

Record payments and send or print documents. Square and Stripe seller-payment integrations are being tested in the controlled beta; availability depends on connected account and supported setup.

Events & craft-show workflows

Organize sales by event, record event expenses, and use offline Show Mode for supported Quick Sale workflows.

Reports & sales tax

Review sales, payments, customers, and events. Export Sales Tax CSVs by finalized invoice/receipt or saved tax component, with date-range and Event filtering. Draft and Void documents are excluded. These exports show tax charged, not necessarily tax collected, refunded, or remitted; they are not a tax filing service.

Data tools & Practice

Import and export supported Catalog/customer data, back up the business, and use a separate Practice area.
