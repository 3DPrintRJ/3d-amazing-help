Create Sale

Create Sale is the starting point for the three customer-facing sales document workflows.

Current beta documentation · Updated September 23, 2026

Invoice

Choose Invoice when you want to create a customer invoice that starts as a Draft and may carry an unpaid balance. After creation, finalize, print/save, email, revise when allowed, and record payments from the Invoice workflow.

Quick Sale

Choose Quick Sale for an immediate paid transaction and Receipt. Customer is optional, so this is the normal choice for walk-in or craft-show sales. You can optionally collect a receipt email without creating a Customer.

Quote

Choose Quote when you want to prepare an estimate before the sale. Quotes can be edited, printed/saved, emailed, and converted to an Invoice if accepted.

Which should I use?

UseBest choice
Customer will pay later or owes a balanceInvoice
Customer pays nowQuick Sale
Customer wants an estimate firstQuote

Customer records

Invoices and Quotes use a Customer record. Quick Sale can use an existing Customer or remain a walk-in transaction.
